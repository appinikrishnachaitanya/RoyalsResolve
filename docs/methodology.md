# Measurement contract v1

## Competition scope
The Round 1 brief specifies IPL and T20 International data. Rankings therefore use retained IPL and T20I deliveries only; other franchise/domestic T20 competitions in the supplied pack are excluded from player scores and candidate eligibility. IPL and T20I remain identifiable in the run model, player sample split, and IPL-only robustness view. A player qualifies separately for batting or bowling after at least 30 pressure deliveries across five matches, 60 ordinary deliveries across five matches, and at least 30 ordinary deliveries across three matches in every format represented in that player's pressure sample. This is the statistical sample rule; it does not require an IPL appearance. A T20I-only player can qualify under the brief but is not thereby proven available for an IPL auction. 2027 auction registration and availability are not verified. RR incumbent coverage and candidate/incumbent comparisons use IPL deliveries only.

This is a runnable research baseline, not a validated auction recommendation. The supplied competition pack is the source; Cricsheet or another upstream provider cannot be established from these files. No proprietary rows are published. Config and code hashes identify the run. Thresholds were specified during implementation before selecting names; this is not a prospectively preregistered study.
## One-page room-for-error formulas
For a chase, p(s) estimates batting task success from score, wickets lost, legal balls remaining, runs needed, need per ball, innings indicator and phase. Batting viability q=p; defending q=1-p. First innings uses the probability of reaching the median total of earlier retained complete first innings. The benchmark does not use the innings being assessed. A venue/season-adjusted benchmark is pending because conservative exclusions make individual venue samples sparse. No venue adjustment is claimed.
Batting stress T consumes one legal ball and adds zero runs. Bowling stress T consumes one legal ball and adds four runs. M(s)=max{k: q(T^j(s))>=tau for every j=0..k}, with k<=min(6, balls remaining). If initial q<tau, M=-1 (already below viability). The safe six-event horizon is 6+; batting and bowling stress units are not averaged. Wicket and boundary shocks are separate pre-outcome probability changes. Terminal target attainment, ten wickets and exhausted legal balls override model predictions. Paths are never interpreted as player choices or causal substitutions.
Hypothesis: tau=0.20, live-contest viability 0.20..0.80, tight tolerance M<=1. Illustrative synthetic path [0.42,0.24,0.18] has M=1; [0.16,...] is already below viability. Threshold checks at 0.15 and 0.25 are fixed-fit sensitivity analyses. Live-bound and tolerance-cutoff settings are declared but broader refit validation is pending. Supported-state extrapolation diagnostics remain incomplete; margin results are experimental.
The strict margin hypothesis fails the two-list coverage requirement in this run. A transparent fallback labels chases with <=60 legal balls left and required rate >=8 runs/over; first innings require <=30 balls and >=3 wickets lost. The same fallback applies to both roles. This is an operational late-demand baseline, not proof of pressure psychology or superiority. Margin-threshold scenarios remain labelled as alternative definitions, not merely changes in a fallback parameter.
## Audit, source semantics and exclusions
Runs are provisionally interpreted as bat runs; team runs are runs+extras1_runs+extras_2_runs. Wides are illegal. Byes and leg-byes are legal and excluded from bowler-charged runs. Overstep/other each carry a one-run extra but lack an authoritative definition: all affected matches are quarantined in the primary analysis, including secondary extras without using nonzero extras_2 alone as a rejection rule. Penalty timing and untyped extras are quarantined. The exclusion is conservative and creates major coverage bias. Alternative no-ball interpretations are not promoted to validated results.
Exclude revised-target/length matches because effective revision timing is absent; no final revised target is projected backwards. Exclude super-over matches, ties/eliminators, abandoned/no-result matches, nonterminal or incomplete innings, too many inferred legal balls and winner-reconciliation failures. Preserve every raw file and its SHA-256 hash. Reconcile retained and excluded row counts; exclusion reasons overlap. Per-match reasons and full schema/null diagnostics remain in private artifacts.
Teams join on competition plus team ID. Match and competition joins require many-to-one validation. XI positions are grouped only when consistent for the same match/team/player; ambiguity becomes missing. Impact substitutes remain separate player IDs and their delivery exposure is retained; starter and substitute are never merged or forced into eleven unique IDs. XI role information is evidence, not a guarantee of actual entry. Source aliases are audited, not silently merged. Original delivery order is by match/innings/innings_ball_num; delivery keys must be unique.
## Six context dimensions and aggregation
Difficulty and resources enter the viability model. Mistake tolerance defines the experimental margin label. Consequence shocks are diagnostic. Six-prior-legal-ball dots/wickets are diagnostic only, avoiding automatic double-counting of state. Environment includes innings/phase and an IPL indicator in run expectations; learned venue, opposition and hierarchical competition/season effects are pending. Missing supported variables stay missing.
The six context dimensions are never summed. For each eligible delivery, batting residual r=bat runs-E[bat runs|context], bowling residual r=E[charged runs|context]-charged runs. The primary ranking explicitly compares pressure residuals with each player's non-pressure contextual residual baseline: pressure lift = 100*(sum pressure residuals - sum over formats[pressure events in format*usual residual per event in that same format])/(pressure events+60). The player's usual baseline is weighted to their pressure IPL/T20I format mix, preventing a format mix change from appearing as pressure lift. Thus positive means the player performed better relative to their own usual contextual contribution; ranks sort this score highest to lowest. The separate pressure-only contextual score is also shown. The 60 pseudo-events shrink the pressure lift toward zero. Eligibility requires 30 pressure deliveries in five matches, 60 non-pressure deliveries across five matches, and 30 non-pressure deliveries across three matches in each format represented in the pressure sample. Expectations are ridge regressions trained on earlier years only, with legal state, innings and IPL context. Striker dismissal and boundary probabilities are separate earlier-year logistic fits. Run-outs are not bowler-credit wickets. No arbitrary wicket bonus, dismissal cost or combined run-equivalent value is asserted.
The fixed zero-centred 60-event prior shrinks small pressure samples; it is transparent fixed-prior partial pooling, not an empirically fitted hierarchical posterior. Match-bootstrap intervals re-estimate the non-pressure baseline within each resample and reflect match-to-match uncertainty conditional on the fitted contextual model. Reference exposure is each player's observed pressure delivery distribution, not a common opponent/venue assignment. Primary rank descends this estimate, then pressure matches descending, player ID ascending. No uncertainty adjustment is silently applied. All estimates are provisional across different roles.
Eligibility: at least 30 pressure delivery events in five independent matches and at least 60 non-pressure deliveries across five matches for the usual baseline. An episode begins on a false-to-true pressure transition, ends when the label becomes false or the innings ends, and is shared across players exposed in that contiguous passage. Match bootstrap resamples all events from an independent match together, 400 seeded replicates. Intervals are percentile intervals conditional on fixed contextual fits; they omit model-fitting uncertainty. Own usual means non-pressure contextual residual. The ranked score is the shrunk pressure-minus-usual lift. The separate pressure-only contextual score and unpooled pressure-minus-usual difference are reported separately. Maintaining a high level is not penalised for a small uplift.
Illustrative synthetic score: 120 pressure events, expected 150 bat runs, observed 168. Residual +18, score=100*18/(120+60)=+10 runs/100. A real reconciliation is printed for every published player in the report and drawer. Wickets, dots, boundaries, extras and uncertainty remain visible beside the score. Early or non-striker dismissals have explicit attribution limitations.
## Role and coach evidence
Role support requires 60 events and five independent matches. Opener evidence uses consistent XI positions 1-2; middle order uses positions 3-5. The "Death overs" batting tag means at least 60 actual batting events across five matches in legal-ball phases 91-120; it describes observed batting exposure, not bowling ability or all-rounder status. Powerplay, middle and death phases are legal balls 1-36, 37-90 and 91-120. Shortened matches are quarantined rather than forcing normal phases onto them.
Entry readiness counts the first six legal balls faced per innings, retaining early dismissals and illegal events before the sixth legal ball. Display bat-run pace on legal balls and dismissal probability per observed innings, stratified by actual first-striker exposure phase and collapse condition. First-striker exposure can lag non-striker arrival; exact arrival timing is not claimed. Collapse requires >=2 wickets in the preceding 12 legal balls at entry. Recovery versus stable-entry residuals describe assignments, not mental toughness. Source position is not used as a reputation label.
Non-boundary scoring = non-boundary bat runs per 100 legal balls, non-boundary scoring-event rate, legal-ball dot rate and boundary share of legal-ball bat runs. Extras never enter bat-run numerators. Wides are not balls faced; legal-ball views are labelled. No claim of actual strike rotation is made.
Bowling damage-control evidence uses all observed delivery events after the first boundary in the same over. Consecutive boundaries remain follow-up opportunities, counted once. Show remaining legal opportunities, actual and expected boundaries and charged runs. Terminal or unfinished overs have fewer follow-up events; this selection bias prevents causal bounce-back claims. Costly exposure reports verified wides per recorded delivery event and legal-ball boundary rate. No-ball rates are unavailable because source no-ball semantics remain unresolved. Event counts are not falsely labelled scheduled overs or measured delivery quality.
Downside is the distribution of independent-match unpooled contextual pressure contributions, including unfavourable matches and losing sides. Show quartile, median and unfavourable counts only with five matches. This variability differs from uncertainty in mean ability. Supporting metrics do not change the primary score.
## Same assignment, different answer
Formal comparisons restrict to common innings/phase/IPL strata, each requiring ten events and three matches per player. Overall overlap requires 30 events and five matches each. Standardise with equal weight over retained shared strata. Bootstrap the union of match clusters jointly, preserving shared-match dependence; report a difference interval and excluded non-overlap events. A stronger case requires the entire interval above or below zero; otherwise inconclusive. These are expected-contribution comparisons, not predictive match-win probabilities. Candidate/incumbent comparisons use the same method. Opponent/venue/position confounds remain, so an interval alone is not an auction upgrade recommendation.
Assignment and alternative scenario switches load saved results. Leave-best-match-out removes the match with greatest total residual; IPL-only, recent 2026 and tau 0.15/0.25 checks are fixed-fit calculations with the same eligibility minimum. These do not refit contextual models. Numerical order reversals are distinct from evidence-supported recommendations. No continuous robustness threshold or fabricated probability is displayed.
## Validation and limits
Rolling annual fits train on all earlier retained years and test on whole later matches. 2022 is warm-up, 2023-2025 development, 2026 later holdout. Linear logistic is the frozen simple reference; a quadratic logistic alternative is evaluated with Brier, log loss and calibration bins. These metrics assess task viability, not the truth of pressure labels. Model metrics are delivery-weighted and retain within-match dependence; no model-superiority claim follows.
Retrospective selection checks compare pooled contextual, conservative lower-bound, ordinary-stat and phase/required-rate lists, measuring later contribution and observed-player attrition. Under the fallback, phase/rate and contextual lists coincide. The checks show mixed outcomes rather than uniform superiority. A holdout first inspected during this build is not a prospectively sealed evaluation; further independent validation is necessary before final submission.
First/chase contributions are shown separately. The provisional overall list combines comparable run-residual units over observed exposure, not viability scales, but the exposure mix is unvalidated. A logistic GAM, empirical transition distribution, contextual wicket values, hierarchical effects, source alternative semantics refits and full prospective validation remain pending. The report and dashboard disclose these rather than inventing results.
